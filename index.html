“Please use this app responsibly. Do not capture or share illegal, harmful, or non-consensual content. Respect privacy, consent, and local laws. You are responsible for ensuring you have permission to photograph people and places.”
-Your GeoStickrs Team

1. Lawful Use Requirement
Users must only use the application in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.
• Users must not use the app to capture, store, or distribute content that violates local, national, or international law.
• The app must not be used for unlawful surveillance, harassment, or stalking.
• Users are responsible for ensuring they have the legal right to capture images of people, locations, and objects.

2. Consent & Privacy (GDPR-aligned)
Respect for privacy is mandatory.
• Users should obtain consent before capturing identifiable individuals where legally required.
• Do not intentionally capture or process images in private spaces where there is a reasonable expectation of privacy (e.g., homes, bathrooms, private offices) without permission.
• The app must not encourage or facilitate covert recording of individuals.
• Users should avoid uploading or sharing images containing sensitive personal data without consent.
If operating in the EU:
• Personal data in images (faces, license plates, identifiable features) is subject to GDPR.
• Users may have obligations to ensure lawful basis for processing (e.g., consent, legitimate interest).

3. Prohibited Content
The app must not be used to capture or distribute:
• Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — strictly prohibited, must be reported and removed
• Non-consensual intimate imagery (including “revenge porn”)
• Content involving exploitation, trafficking, or abuse
• Content that facilitates violence or harm toward individuals or groups
• Content that identifies private individuals for harassment or targeting (doxxing)

4. Harassment & Harm Prevention
Users must not:
• Use the app to harass, threaten, or intimidate others
• Take photos with the intent to shame, blackmail, or exploit individuals
• Record individuals in vulnerable situations (medical emergencies, distress) without a legitimate reason

5. Public Safety Restrictions
Users must not use the app in ways that endanger themselves or others:
• No use while driving, cycling, or operating machinery in a dangerous manner
• No interference with emergency services or restricted areas (e.g., crime scenes, military zones)
• Do not obstruct law enforcement or emergency personnel

6. Sensitive Locations
Users should respect restrictions and social norms in sensitive areas:
• Healthcare facilities (hospitals, clinics)
• Religious sites
• Schools and childcare environments
• Government or secure facilities
• Private property where photography is prohibited
Users are responsible for checking and following posted signage and local rules.

7. Intellectual Property Rights
• Users must not capture or distribute copyrighted works in violation of applicable law.
• This includes artworks, performances, branded content, or proprietary materials where photography is restricted.
• Users are responsible for ensuring they have rights to use and share captured images.

8. Enforcement Policy
Violations may result in:
• Content removal
• Temporary suspension of features
• Reporting to relevant authorities where legally required (especially in cases involving CSAM, threats, or serious illegal activity)

9. User Responsibility Clause
Users acknowledge that:
• They are solely responsible for how they use the camera features
• The application provider is not liable for misuse of captured content
• They must comply with all applicable laws in their jurisdiction
